Security Center

See what your PC is doing.

A clear look at your protection, what is connected to the internet, what is listening for connections, and what starts with Windows. Plinth reports facts and leaves the judgement to you.

This is visibility, not an antivirus. Plinth does not scan files and cannot tell you a program is safe or malicious. A program appearing here is not a sign of a problem by itself.

Wallpapers

Motion that respects your PC.

Pictures from your own folders, live video and animated pages, on exactly the monitors you choose. Live wallpapers run in a locked box: they can draw, but they cannot reach the internet, your files or your widgets. They pause by themselves during games, on low battery and in remote sessions.

Moods and setups

Your desktop follows your day.

Moods switch between your saved profiles by rules you write: the program you are using, a fullscreen game, battery, or the time of day. Plinth never fights a choice you make by hand, and it only suggests a rule after you repeat the same choice.

Rules

Profiles are saved on the Wallpapers page. The first matching rule wins; there is a two minute pause between switches so nothing flickers.

Network

Widgets never talk to the internet directly.

All widget traffic is forced through a Plinth proxy that blocks everything except hosts a widget declared and you allowed. A widget with no network permission can reach nothing at all.
